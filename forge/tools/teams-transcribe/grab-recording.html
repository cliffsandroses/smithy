Grab recording

One click on a Teams recording sends it to the homelab for screenshots and a transcript.

Set up once: show the favourites bar in Edge (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it:

Grab recording
  1. Open a recording on SharePoint / Stream (the page that plays it).
  2. Click Grab recording on the favourites bar.
  3. Say what it's for: 1 PNIP, 2 you, 3 team meeting.
  4. The first time, pick the folder EC01 \ PCAI \ inbox. It's remembered after that.
  5. A green note says what was saved: the Teams transcript (when the meeting had one) and the video link. The homelab does the rest within a few minutes.

If it can't save into the folder it downloads the two files instead: move them into EC01\PCAI\inbox.
